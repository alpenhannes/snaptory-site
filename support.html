Common questions

A card scanned as the wrong card.

Snaptory matches on the printed artwork, so different prints of the same artwork are the hardest case - a reverse holo and its regular version share an image, and some reprints are identical apart from a set symbol. Tap "that's not right" on the result to pick from the near matches. If a specific card is reliably wrong, please report it.

Scanning is not recognising anything.

Fill the on-screen frame with the card, keep it flat and roughly square to the camera, and avoid direct glare on holo cards - reflections change the artwork the match relies on. Sleeved cards work, but a glossy or heavily scuffed sleeve makes it harder.

A price looks wrong or is missing.

Prices come from a market snapshot that refreshes once a day, so a card that moved sharply today may lag until tomorrow. Some cards have no price at all: TCGplayer does not list every card individually, and sealed-product-only promos in particular often have no single-card market price to report.

Do I need an internet connection?

No scan waits on one. Card recognition is entirely on-device, and Snaptory ships with card names and a price snapshot built in, so it keeps up on the busy wifi at a card show. A connection lets it fetch card images, refresh prices, pick up newly released sets, and open a card's listings page.

Where is my collection stored? Is it backed up?

On your iPhone, and only there. Snaptory has no account and no cloud sync, which is why it needs no personal data - but it also means deleting the app deletes your collection, and it cannot be recovered. See the privacy policy.

Will you add Android, iPad, or cloud sync?

Snaptory is iPhone-only for now. Feature requests are welcome by email.
