Privacy Policy

Snaptory - Last updated 4 October 2026

Snaptory does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking. The only thing that ever reaches us is a feedback message you choose to send.

Your collection stays on your device

Everything you record in Snaptory - the cards you own, their condition, quantity, what you paid, and any collections you build - is stored in a database on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers that hold your data, because there are none to hold it.

Your inventory, collections and piles are included in your device's own iCloud or Google backup. Exports are created only when you tap Export, and go only where you send them. We never receive either, which also means we cannot recover your data for you.

Camera images never leave your phone

Snaptory identifies cards by comparing them against a catalogue of card fingerprints that ships inside the app. That comparison happens entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device vision framework. Live camera frames are processed in memory. The optional photo fallback writes a temporary app file and deletes it after processing. Images are never saved to your photo library, uploaded, or sent to any third party.

Snaptory requests camera access for this and only this. It does not request access to your photo library, contacts, location, or microphone.

Feedback you send

If you use Send feedback in the More tab, the text you write is sent together with the app version, your platform and OS version, and the version of the card catalog on your device. Nothing else is attached: no name, email address, account, collection data, or device identifier. The message passes through a small Cloudflare Worker that we operate and is delivered to a private Discord channel that only the developer reads. Cloudflare and Discord process it under their own privacy policies, and Cloudflare sees your IP address as with any request. Do not put personal details in a message unless you want us to have them. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

Network requests the app makes

Snaptory needs card names, images, and market prices, which it fetches from the public sources below. These requests carry no personal information and no identifier for you or your device. As with any request over the internet, the receiving service can see your IP address; each is governed by its own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat it is used for
images.pokemontcg.io, images.scrydex.com, assets.tcgdex.net Card images shown in the app.
github.com Shared daily price and history files, plus versioned catalog, scanner, and marketplace-map files. These cover the whole catalog rather than querying per card, so no request reveals which cards you own.
snaptory-feedback.steffen-appelhans.workers.dev (Cloudflare), then Discord Only when you tap Send in the feedback sheet: your message and the version details described above.

What Snaptory does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address required.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No crash- or performance-reporting service.
  • No selling or sharing of data. Feedback you send is read by the developer and never sold.

Children

Snaptory is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Snaptory changes what it collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Should Snaptory ever collect personal data, you would be asked first.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Snaptory can be sent through the Snaptory support request page. Requests are public, so do not include personal or sensitive information.
