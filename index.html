Snaptory

Point your camera at a Pokémon card and know what it is, and what it's worth.

Recognises cards on your phone

Card recognition runs entirely on your iPhone against a catalogue built into the app - no upload, and no scan waiting on a connection. Useful where you actually need it: at a card show, where the wifi cannot keep up with you.

Daily market prices

Every card is matched to market data that refreshes once a day, with a 30-day history so you can see which way a card has moved.

Your binder, organised

Browse English and Japanese cards. Record Cardmarket condition, quantity, grading, and what you paid. Build collections and track cards or every variant.

No account, no tracking

Snaptory has no sign-in and collects nothing. Your collection lives on your phone and stays there.
